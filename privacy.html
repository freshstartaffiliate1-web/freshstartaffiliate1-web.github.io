Privacy Policy

Fresh Start Uploader · Effective October 13, 2026

Who we are

Fresh Start Uploader is a private tool operated by Fresh Start Affiliate. It is used only by the channel owner to upload their own videos to their own YouTube channels.

What data the app accesses

When you sign in with Google and grant permission, the app uses the YouTube Data API to:

  • upload videos and set their title, description, tags, privacy status and publish time;
  • set video thumbnails and upload captions;
  • add uploaded videos to playlists on the same channel.

The app does not read your email, contacts, comments, watch history or any other Google account data.

How data is stored

Sign-in tokens are stored only on the operator's own computer, so the app can upload without asking you to sign in every time. They are not uploaded to any server, database or third party.

How data is shared

We do not sell, rent or share any user data. Data is sent only to YouTube (Google) to perform the uploads you request.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Fresh Start Uploader's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app also uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

You can remove this app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access stops all further uploads. Locally stored tokens can be deleted by the operator on request.
